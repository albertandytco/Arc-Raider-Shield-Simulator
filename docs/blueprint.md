# **App Name**: Shield Assault

## Core Features:

- Damage Calculation: Calculates damage to shield and life based on the selected weapon and shield mitigation.
- Shielded Health Display: Presents the shield and life bars with values for 'Light Shield' (Green), 'Medium Shield' (Blue), and 'Heavy Shield' (Pink).
- Damage Tracking: Displays the damage received by the shield and life separately for each shield type.
- Fire Button: A 'Fire' button that triggers damage calculation and shield/life reduction upon press. Includes weapons: Ferro (40), Chaleira (10), Matraca (9), Costurador (7), Toro (67.5), Grampo (20), Burleta (10), Bigorna (40).
- Reset Button: A 'Reset' button to restore shield and life values to their initial states.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft grey-blue (#B0C4DE) to set a serious, technological tone.
- Background color: Light grey (#F0F8FF) to ensure readability and a clean aesthetic.
- Accent colors: Green (#90EE90) for 'Light Shield', Blue (#ADD8E6) for 'Medium Shield', and Pink (#FFB6C1) for 'Heavy Shield' to provide visual cues.
- Font: 'Inter' sans-serif font for all text, to ensure a modern and readable interface.
- Clearly segmented layout with shield bars and damage indicators neatly organized. Ensure bars have the colors asked by user for better identifiaction.
